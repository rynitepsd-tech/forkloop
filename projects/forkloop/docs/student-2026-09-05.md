**Reading the histogram before the rate.** This is not a 0 % on the task; it is a 0 % on the action format. The
base model was trained for a browser harness with `visit_url`, and it reaches for it the moment the task says
"OpenEMR at http://localhost/openemr": 10 rejected `visit_url` calls end 24 of the 30 episodes at the env's
invalid-action limit, typically inside 30 steps, before any of them has logged into OpenEMR. Between the rejections
the actions are sane and on-task (click the omnibox, `ctrl+l`, `alt+d`, the OpenEMR login fields at the right
coordinates, `admin` typed into the username field) — the model simply never types a URL by hand. The other six
episodes stopped themselves with Fara's critical-point behaviour (`ask_user_question` → `done(success=false)`), five
of them within 20 steps. Zero wrong-record, zero collateral edits, zero transcription/decoy errors: nothing got far
enough to make those mistakes. Gate 1 fires (invalid rate ≫ 5 %), so the parser was fixed and the same 30 seeds
re-run once (step 3b).

**The fix** (`forkloop/policies/student.py`, `--nav-macro`, commit 2fa1a77, 204 offline tests): the policy
expands `visit_url` into the contract's own navigation, click(640, 90) → key(ctrl+a) → type(url) → key(Return),
over four env steps with one model call (the queued three are marked `macro` in the step meta), and `history_back`
into key(alt+Left); the tool schema advertises both. This is exactly the sequence the teacher prompts spell out and
the scripted GUI fixture uses; the contract action space is unchanged.
